## RISEx airdrop: RISE points and how to farm them
URL: https://sypherscore.com/risex-airdrop

Status: RISEx has no token yet. Its docs say 100% of RISE points are allocated to RISEx users — traders, LPs and builder-code integrators. Season 0 retro points went out on 23 July 2026; Season 1 "Ignite" runs from 20 July 2026 until no later than Q2 2027.

Steps: 1) Sign up on RISEx with an invite code. 2) Trade with limit orders and keep real positions open — holding time is scored. 3) Keep costs low: slippage and bad fills hurt your score. 4) Track your weekly share every Tuesday; RISEx Passport shows your on-chain stats.
